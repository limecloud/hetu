# Return tomorrow without explaining the whole work again

Last night you decided the character should stop outside the door because he recognized a voice. Today a new conversation has him walk straight in.

The thing to continue is a creative choice, not merely a line in a chat.

## Save enough to continue

The useful material may be one passage, the pause you chose and the instruction not to reveal the speaker. The whole conversation, every rejected idea and temporary scripts need not become project content.

When you ask to keep the work, the Agent saves an independent draft and its necessary sources through the machine interface. A new conversation can find the actual material; an earlier claim of completion is not proof of storage.

## A revision with clear boundaries

> Move their conflict earlier in chapter two. Keep the reconciliation in chapter three and the line we chose yesterday.

Locate the relevant versions. Read only the chapters and references needed for the change. Point out concrete conflicts between requirements. Return a draft that can be read continuously and compared with the original.

This does not promise complete automatic impact detection across a novel. Recorded revisions and references can be checked deterministically; motivation and dramatic effect require judgment.

## Keep the work when the assistant changes

An Agent can continue from the draft, public feedback and unfinished scope without copying the entire chat. Verify engine thread recovery separately; terminal output is not evidence of a saved work.

Keep ordinary manuscripts and selected exports retrievable. Explain the actual formats and dependencies of professional projects. The benefit is one less explanation and one less accidental rewrite.
