One letter can appear in a shot, inspire a place name, be sung by two characters or become evidence in a mystery. Each form needs its own structures and evidence to support another revision.

## What changed between two takes

[Script Supervisor](/en/plugins/script-supervisor/) keeps shots, takes and explicit continuity observations. Was the envelope moved deliberately, or does the scene need another take? Compare both records first. Missing observations remain unknown, and later corrections preserve the original.

## Why a name is written this way

[Constructed Language Studio](/en/plugins/language-studio/) connects phonemes, orthography, roots and ordered rules. Inspect the steps behind place names and inhabitants’ titles, with word-by-word evidence for examples. Changed rules leave earlier names available for explicit review; authors judge culture and aesthetics.

## Why a character sings now

[Musical Theatre Studio](/en/plugins/musical-studio/) places dialogue, songs, vocal parts and reprises in performance order. Two simultaneous singers do not double the duration. Unknown tempo stays unknown. Rehearsals retain their complete original version so programs can be compared after song changes.

## From complete role books to playtesting

[Murder Mystery Studio](/en/plugins/murder-mystery/) v0.2.0 preserves earlier projects and playtests while adding complete staged role texts, declared knowledge, evidence deductions and mechanics. Searches have budgets, exchanges require held clues, and votes and reveals follow fixed rounds. Ties do not arbitrarily choose an ending, and player material reflects actual releases.

Agent simulation can expose conditions, blockers and revision needs. It cannot prove human experience or turn a conversation that has read host material into an isolated player. Read complete books, consider human feedback and start a new playtest after revision.

Tell the Agent your goal, return to read the complete result and name a specific change. Professional projects keep their own evidence while continuing around the same work.
