# Turn a page, or give the story another reader

Lin opens an envelope. Inside lies an old key.

In a novel, the next paragraph might explain its origin. In a picturebook, the key can occupy a page of its own, with the old station revealed after the turn. In a murder mystery, Lin may know the key while the station keeper holds a register she has not seen.

The source story is shared. The creative structures differ.

## The page turn tells part of the story

[Picturebook Studio](/en/plugins/picturebook/) keeps pages, spreads, actual images, text frames and page-turn intentions in one project. Ask your Agent to leave a question on page two, then read the complete spreads and identify the page to revise.

The software checks physical dimensions, bleed, safe areas, actual font metrics and effective image resolution. Missing glyphs and overflowing text block PDF delivery. Offline HTML supports reading; embedded-font PDF preserves the measured layout. Printer approval and real children’s reading remain separate judgments.

## Each role holds a different story

[Murder Mystery Studio](/en/plugins/murder-mystery/) separates author material, host manuals and role packets. Packets are constructed for an explicit role and act, excluding complete project JSON, other roles’ secrets and future clues.

A playtest starts from a fixed author revision. Releases, reasons for skipping a clue, guesses and the debrief remain in sequence. Required clues must be handled before advancing. Later author revisions preserve the earlier test.

This is an authoring and offline recording tool. Agent simulations are labeled clearly, do not stand in for human research and do not send anything to players automatically.

## Carry the debrief into the next draft

Export is one step in a continuing project. Ask your Agent to read page feedback or a playtest debrief and prepare a new candidate in the source work. Keep prior evidence; adoption and finalization remain author decisions.

Install the tools that fit your task. Let specialized structures grow around your work while each revision retains a basis for the next.
