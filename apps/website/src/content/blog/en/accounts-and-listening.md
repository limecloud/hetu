Two people remember the same station but give different closing years. Their disagreement can become a beginning: place both accounts together and listen to the questions each raises.

## Preserve the disagreement before choosing the story

[Documentary Studio](/en/plugins/documentary-studio/) keeps original words, actual media, claims, support and counterevidence distinct. Read a complete paper edit, compare openings and play the source media. Unrecorded narration carries an estimate; real excerpts retain their source ranges for production.

A changed claim may require another reading of an earlier paper edit. Earlier versions and feedback remain available so the author can review what changed before fixing a new basis.

## A revised script needs another listen

[Podcast Studio](/en/plugins/podcast-studio/) arranges an episode around its host script and actual recordings. Excerpts, pauses and sections form playable audio, with chapter times derived from that output. A changed script leaves the original recording intact and identifies lines still awaiting recording.

Tell your Agent the goal, return to read or listen through the work, then give feedback on a specific passage. Documentary paper edits and podcast episodes can inform one another while retaining their own projects and author decisions.


## Use only what this work needs

Develop only what needs testing now. Sources, scripts and assets retain their revisions. Trying one scene needs no whole-book conversion; existing clips need not be generated again. State what changes and what remains before continuing.
