# More than one way to write a life

One record gives Lin Xi's birth year as 1980; another says 1982. Accounts of the station's closure differ too. A creative project can begin with those questions without silently settling them.

## A poem keeps its distance

[Poetry Studio](/en/plugins/poetry-studio/) preserves complete poems, stanzas and reasons for line alternatives. Free verse needs no mandatory metre. Explicit form checks retain the system, source and positional readings, keeping uncertainty visible. Revised lines leave old annotations available for review.

## An article keeps its questions

[Nonfiction Studio](/en/plugins/nonfiction-studio/) connects article claims with exact interviews, support and counterevidence. An assessment records its reasoning against a fixed draft and evidence set. A revised ending does not silently inherit an earlier verification result.

## A biography keeps its unfinished knowledge

[Biography Studio](/en/plugins/biography-studio/) distinguishes namesakes and preserves date precision. A year remains a year rather than an invented day. Chapters retain the life events behind them, so new material identifies what deserves another reading.

The three projects can continue around one work while retaining separate judgments. Ask your Agent to prepare the work, return to read and compare it, then name a specific line to revisit. The engineering preserves evidence; expression and judgment remain the author's.


## Use only what this work needs

A quick idea or read-only question can end with an answer. Keep sources, feedback and revisions for work you intend to continue, without turning every discussion into a project task. Delegate or write directly; consequential choices refer to concrete results.
