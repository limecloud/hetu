## “Keep the ending. Rework the argument.”

You have read a proposed chapter. The final image works: a light still burning at the ferry landing. The argument before it explains too much. You know what you want to keep and what needs another pass.

Finding a file, copying the passage, repeating the character notes and filling in another revision form should not consume that moment of judgment.

With Hetu Luoshu, you give the instruction to the external Agent you already use. It returns to the same task, reads the relevant draft and feedback, and prepares another revision. In the desktop app, you compare the result and decide whether it belongs in the story.

## Start with an idea

“I want to write about a ferry landing before its final crossing. I don't have a title yet.”

That is enough to begin a conversation. Your Agent helps explore the protagonist's goal, what the closure changes and what can sustain the story beyond its opening. It organizes possible titles, a synopsis and an outline into alternatives you can compare.

The Agent prepares the structured project details. You review the direction and proposed location for the work, without retyping the conversation into a creation form. Within the explicit creation scope, it creates the project and prepares the next task.

A novel, an original screenplay and an adaptation have different starting points. An original screenplay can begin with characters and episodes. An adaptation keeps its source and version clear. Neither requires inventing an empty novel to satisfy a project form.

## Open the app to make creative decisions

The desktop app puts visual review first: read a draft, compare openings, inspect an adaptation against its source, examine references and cover candidates, or watch previews and edits with their sound and subtitles.

Receiving a candidate gives you an option. Adopting one passage changes the working manuscript through your decision. Finalizing confirms a particular saved revision separately.

You can keep an ending, reject a new character, adopt a paragraph or write your own version after reading the alternatives. Direct editing is always available, whether you want to adjust one line or write the entire work yourself.

## Continue from the page you are on

Novels, scripts and videos are independent works in the creative library. Chapters, references, assets and delivery follow the current work, with fixed source links where needed. Each deserves a clear way to involve your Agent within its own work and task scope.

| What needs attention | A goal to give your Agent | What you review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chapter order | “Move the ferry argument to the start of part two. Preserve the text.” | The directory changes |
| Conflicting references | “Organize these notes and keep the differing accounts visible.” | Sources, conflicts and proposed choices |
| A cover revision | “Keep the rainy landing, but make the character smaller.” | Proportional previews and alternatives |
| A script handoff | “Prepare the finalized chapters, episode scripts and character references.” | Selected versions, included material and export results |

The current page provides a contextual handoff. An existing task continues with its draft, revisions and feedback. Your Agent prepares the inputs the software needs, rather than asking you to learn and fill every form.

## Give the Agent a direct way to work

Skills supply creative methods. MCP and CLI let your external Agent query the workspace, prepare operations, perform scoped work and read back the result. The native app presents the same manuscripts, references, tasks and author decisions.

This reduces the need to find buttons, move text between windows and reconstruct what happened. After an interruption, the next step is to recover the existing work and operation. A new conversation does not automatically mean a new book or another duplicate submission.

When an operation needs your decision, the Agent prepares the complete proposal. Hetu presents its effect so you can review that specific change. Approval stays tied to that proposal; it does not grant access to your whole library or approve the next revision.

## Leave room for the author's writing

Project details, organization and delivery preparation can move through tools. Whether a character should stay silent, whether an ending says too much and whether a line sounds like your work remain decisions worth spending time on.

Hetu does not put another conversational Agent inside the app. You keep working with your chosen client, then return to a place where its contributions can be understood, compared and developed further.

> As AI grows more capable, let it work with the body of work you build over time. Let every generated draft become something you can understand, choose from and continue developing.

## Use only what this work needs

A quick idea or read-only question can end with an answer. Keep sources, feedback and revisions for work you intend to continue, without turning every discussion into a project task. Delegate or write directly; consequential choices refer to concrete results.
