## “Hear the footsteps before we see the person.”

That single comment can change a scene’s suspense. The immediate question is when sound enters and when the audience learns someone is outside. It may not yet require final casting, costumes or lighting.

If polished generation comes first, a misplaced cut can make beautiful footage expensive to revise. Use material that lets you inspect the decision you are actually making.

## Different material answers different questions

Read the script to judge motivation and whether dialogue reveals too much. Use an animatic with temporary sound to explore pauses, cuts and reveals.

When someone must move around a table, avoid a sightline or pass a camera through a narrow space, a simple 3D preview can help with paths, occlusion and staging. Ordinary dialogue does not require a 3D scene for every shot.

To inspect consistent identity, achievable movement, acting or lip sync, you need representative actual clips. A proxy preview cannot establish those qualities.

[LibTV’s public production methods](https://github.com/liblib-ai/marketplace/tree/4ac581c3cbdaa53d0d42df85c6bf62e45600bc5f) distinguish the roles of proxy motion, environment images and character images. Hetu chooses preview material around the question being judged; final production still needs its own checks.

## A red proxy is not a final red costume

A preview may use colors to distinguish characters and blocks to represent furniture. Those choices describe identity and space within the preview. They are not final wardrobe or environment requirements.

Ask your Agent to distinguish reference roles: the proxy describes movement and timing, a character reference describes identity and clothing, and a location reference describes appearance. Approving the preview does not guarantee a model will reproduce every frame or movement.

## Watch continuously and return feedback to the task

Watch with neighboring shots and sound first. Then pause to inspect a character, a turn or a subtitle. You can comment anywhere rather than only on moments selected by the Agent.

Try: “Keep the first half. Hold longer after the door opens.” Let the Agent retain the version and interval, revise the external project and return an actual playable result. Earlier comments stay with the earlier edit; the new cut deserves another viewing.

## Revise the layer where the problem occurs

For motivation, revisit the script. For staging, revise the preview. For appearance, check character references. For an early sound cue, change the audio and edit before regenerating all the picture.

Before generation, agree on outgoing content, model mode, duration, sound and spending scope. Check unknown results on the original task. Downloading, complete decoding and author approval are separate evidence, each with its own purpose.

Before delivery, verify the confirmed video along with source media, subtitles, audio and an editable project. A useful preview helps you see the problem, choose a direction and continue the work.
