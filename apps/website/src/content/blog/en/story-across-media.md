# More ways for one story to continue

An unsigned letter reaches Lin at the old post office. A novel can stay with her thoughts. A script needs a visible action. A comic must choose whether we see the envelope or her hand first. An audio drama might let the rain fall silent.

Each form makes its own choices. A useful tool keeps their sources and makes revision possible.

## Explain the adaptation

[Adaptation Studio](/en/plugins/adaptation-studio/) connects fixed source units with a target structure. Record why a monologue disappears, two waits merge or a meeting happens earlier. Keep read, partly read and unread material distinct.

The target remains a separate work. Adaptation exceptions do not rewrite the original. Preview affected mappings before updating scripts or media.

## Keep feedback with its draft

[Reader Review](/en/plugins/reader-review/) creates an offline reading packet. Readers download their completed feedback and return the file; the project preserves their words and declared coverage. Human readers and Agent simulations remain distinct, with editorial decisions stored separately.

A question may suggest a revision or a deliberate mystery. Read the actual new draft before resolving an old concern.

## Read the page and hear the scene

[Comic Studio](/en/plugins/comic-studio/) organizes pages, panels, direction, balloons and actual images. Check order and missing art, then read the rendered page.

[Audio Drama](/en/plugins/audio-drama/) brings roles, lines, actual recordings and a timeline together. Trims, silence, overlaps, pan and fades are deterministic; performance still needs listening. Review notes stay bound to their original timeline and need rechecking after edits.

Ask your Agent to perform the work through Hetu. Return to read, listen and name the part to change, or edit directly. External materials, paid voice work and final approval remain separate decisions.

## Combine tools as needed

These tools work independently or pass fixed references within a work. They do not require four separate assistant plugins or merge novels, scripts and media into a record that overwrites itself.

Choose a form in the [plugin center](/en/plugins/) and describe your goal with the [guide](/en/docs/plugins/). Each saved result gives the next revision a place to begin.


## Use only what this work needs

Develop only what needs testing now. Sources, scripts and assets retain their revisions. Trying one scene needs no whole-book conversion; existing clips need not be generated again. State what changes and what remains before continuing.
