# From the plan to the next draft

Twenty chapters into a novel, the next task may be choosing a volume’s direction, making dialogue more restrained or fixing a character who knows a secret too early. Each task needs a different method, connected to the same work.

## Read the past before planning the future

Novel Planner keeps book, volume and chapter structure alongside detailed plots, and adds direction comparisons, separate story and narrative timelines, and character states. Your Agent reads existing material, states its actual coverage and compares choices and costs. Unknown times remain unknown. Choosing a direction does not adopt manuscript text.

## Preferences need evidence; concerns need rechecking

[Prose Studio](/en/plugins/prose-studio/) separates stated preferences from Agent inferences. Compare openings or refine a fixed selection while verifying unchanged bytes outside it. Preferences can be disabled.

[Novel Review](/en/plugins/novel-review/) shows coverage before concerns. Unchecked, inconclusive and passed remain distinct. A new draft alone does not resolve an issue: the Agent reads the new fixed candidate and records the recheck evidence.

## One story, several views

[Novel Atlas](/en/plugins/novel-atlas/) organizes people, factions, relationships, places, events, changing states and objects from shared evidence. Canonical projections, extracted candidates and future plans remain distinct. A tidy diagram does not establish geography or correctness.

Offline HTML remains readable without scripts and excludes full chapters by default. Saved professional data remains when a plugin is disabled.

## Connect research, characters and analysis

[Story Research](/en/plugins/story-research/) organizes questions, observations, support and counterevidence. [Character Lab](/en/plugins/character-lab/) develops motivation hypotheses, interviews, dialogue trials and arcs. [Story Analysis](/en/plugins/story-analysis/) tracks reading coverage, scenes, signals and evidence-linked findings. Use each independently or connect them around the same novel.

Ask your Agent to research why Lin stays at the station, separate source facts from inferences and counterevidence, compare two motivations in Character Lab, then analyze their consequences for the next chapter. Read existing book, volume and chapter plans before preparing a candidate. Revise from feedback and read the new draft before rechecking the concern. Keep fixed handoff evidence; canon, adoption and finalization remain your decisions.

Review sources before conclusions, distinguish facts from hypotheses, and check reading coverage before findings. Simulated readers are not real reader feedback, name mentions do not establish appearances, and a new draft alone does not resolve a concern.

## You do not need to operate every tool

Give your Agent a goal. It discovers installed Hetu plugins, reads their packaged methods and works through the shared machine interface. Return to read plans, compare candidates, inspect sources and give feedback. Adoption and finalization remain your decisions.

Choose only what helps the current task; these tools do not require one another. Explore the [plugin center](/en/plugins/) or the [author guide](/en/docs/plugins/).

## More forms for the same work

[Lorebook Bridge](/en/plugins/lorebook-bridge/) preserves original card files. [Interactive Fiction](/en/plugins/interactive-fiction/) offers playable routes, while [Series Organizer](/en/plugins/series-organizer/) organizes sequels and side stories. [Story Translation](/en/plugins/story-translation/) aligns translation feedback to fixed passages, [Readthrough](/en/plugins/readthrough/) connects recordings to text, and [Book Export](/en/plugins/book-export/) compiles chosen versions into readable files.

The tools share fixed sources rather than forcing you to enter the same material again. Review actual results and decide what follows. See the six workflows and conversation examples in the [plugin guide](/en/docs/plugins/).

## Continue the next chapter with its reasons intact

[Novel Planner](/en/plugins/novel-planner/) records explicit event order, simultaneity and time intervals, keeping learning, belief and forgetting separate. [Prose Studio](/en/plugins/prose-studio/) selects preferences for the current character, chapter and scene. A chosen revision can become a learning sample; a single experiment does not become a permanent rule.

[Novel Review](/en/plugins/novel-review/) checks fixed text against author dictionaries, finds exact repeated sentences and examines evidenced balances, objects, character states and knowledge. Missing premises remain inconclusive. [Novel Atlas](/en/plugins/novel-atlas/) presents maps, routes, numerical curves or qualitative stages, directional relationships and object transfers, with unread chapters and unknown positions visible.

Ask your Agent to review earlier volumes, prepare the next chapter, revise a chosen passage, recheck knowledge and object changes, and update the atlas. Each handoff keeps its fixed version. Adoption and finalization remain your decisions.
