# When an Agent and a folder are enough

“Give me three titles” can be answered in the conversation you already use. Creating a project, completing a genre form and installing plugins would cost more effort than the answer saves.

A task completed without Hetu can be the right outcome.

## Start with the problem you actually have

The need often appears on the second or fifth revision. A new conversation forgets why a character must not apologize. A useful line disappears during a plot change. The version sent to a collaborator no longer matches the one on your desk.

Hetu should help retain the drafts, feedback and choices needed to continue that work.

## Three useful levels of involvement

| What you need | A suitable path |
| --- | --- |
| Try a line, explain a term or edit an ordinary file | Use your current Agent and original file |
| Find a character detail or earlier choice | Read only what is needed and answer with references |
| Continue a long work, compare drafts, revise or deliver | Keep the relevant revisions and sources with the work |

Move between these paths as needed. Save a scene when you decide it is worth developing. Stop after a read-only answer when no change is required.

## Delegation and direct writing both belong

AI-first means less manual entry and operation. Your Agent can continue within a clear scope while software handles reliable storage. A decision already made in conversation needs no second round of clicks.

Execution and creative acceptance remain distinct. A saved alternative is not automatically adopted; a final is the particular version you chose.

## Test the benefit with a small revision

Choose a passage and say what should change and what must remain. Compare the effort of finding, explaining and correcting. If your existing tools suffice, keep using them.
