# From a world’s rules to a line on screen

“Three days of harbour closure” affects supplies, sailings and when a character receives news. Revealing a key in chapter two changes what readers can infer. On screen, the outline of a raincoat and the duration of a subtitle become creative choices too.

Authors need a clear way to express those choices. An external Agent organizes the project; Hetu makes its sources and results available to read and watch.

## Make the premises visible

[World Studio](/en/plugins/world-studio/) preserves explicit fictional calendars, rule dependencies and resource units. A calculation reports what remains after three days of rations and which premises are only hypotheses.

A shortfall can start the next scene. It does not establish historical realism or automatically become canon. Inspect affected scenarios before changing a rule; old revisions remain readable.

## Separate author truth from reader knowledge

[Mystery Lab](/en/plugins/mystery-lab/) distinguishes truth, original testimony, clue reliability and reveal stages. A chapter-two reader view contains only evidence revealed by that stage.

A compatible hypothesis is not a unique solution. Future clues cannot silently support it, and testimony is not automatically fact. Whether the mystery feels fair or compelling remains a creative judgment.

## View the images and play the subtitles

[Art Direction](/en/plugins/art-direction/) organizes actual fixed images, crops, palettes and design alternatives. Character references resolve to a specific canon entity. Region notes belong to an exact image; replacing it preserves old notes and calls for rechecking.

[Subtitle Studio](/en/plugins/subtitle-studio/) brings fixed media and bilingual subtitles together. Preview import losses, preserve originals and play the media to check content, timing and reading load. Recheck notes after shifts, then verify SRT, VTT or basic ASS exports with their conversion notes.

## Carry the results into the next chapter

Ask your Agent: “Check the closure dates and rations without revealing the sender. Use my actual reference images for costumes, verify the dialogue subtitles, and carry the constraints into a chapter candidate.”

Each tool owns its professional project and references fixed results. Novels, scripts and video keep independent identities. Upstream changes do not overwrite downstream work, and candidates do not imply adoption or final approval. Tools extend expression while authors retain the decisions.
