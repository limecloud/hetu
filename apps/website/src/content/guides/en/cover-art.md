# Create and choose a cover

A cover can begin with a clear feeling. Ask your external Agent to prepare directions and candidates, then enlarge and compare the results in Hetu.

Generating an image, receiving a candidate and choosing the current cover are separate steps. A new candidate does not automatically replace your cover.

## Describe the story's tone and use

> Prepare cover directions for this small-town mystery. The daughter stands outside the watch shop at night, with a little warm light inside. Emphasize waiting and uncertainty without horror monsters. Suggest two compositions, keep the figure modest in size and leave clear room for the title. Explain the tool and cost before generating.

Give the Agent the title, author name, genre and intended use. Provide an authorized synopsis and character details if useful; the image service does not need the entire novel.

If the title is unsettled, compare artwork without text first. Changing a book title does not update lettering already embedded in an old image.

## Check material and budget before generation

The Agent should identify the tool, text and reference images it will send, number of candidates and cost ceiling. Confirm that specific scope in the current conversation before execution.

For Hetu's optional service, check what the selected model actually supports. Text and image choices are separate; external tools also depend on their current capabilities. See [Model services and costs](model-services.md).

Query the original request when the outcome is unknown. Do not create another paid task to test the result. If no image tool is available, retain the design proposal and the specific missing capability.

## Compare candidates in Hetu

Open the cover area in the work profile and enlarge the original. Look at the overall tone, then the character, details and text.

- Does the title remain readable at library thumbnail size?
- Does the person fit this book rather than a generic poster?
- Are hands, clocks and door frames credible?
- Is the lettering correct, or would clean artwork with separate typography work better?

For feedback: “Keep the second image's light, make the figure smaller and remove the incorrect lettering. Preserve the shop's layout.” Continue the same task with its references.

## Choose the current cover

Use the candidate's cover-selection action available in your app version. Return to the work profile and library to confirm the actual result.

A candidate is not a formal cover selection. Selection preserves versions, and cover history retains old artwork and presentation. To reuse an old design, select it as the current version without overwriting history.

If two windows change a cover, refresh and compare the latest selection before proceeding. An old view should not overwrite another decision.

## Preparation images and local files

Cover candidates from book preparation can be checked and brought into the created work. Importing preserves their original request and images without generating again; choosing the formal cover remains a separate decision.

For an existing local image, use the local import option in cover management and check the original and crop. Importing an existing file does not require a new paid generation.

Finally, check image rights and delivery permissions. Being able to preview an image does not establish permission to publish it or transfer its original file.
