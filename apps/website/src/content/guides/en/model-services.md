# Model services and costs

## Start with what you need

Use suitable tools already available to your Agent. Choose Hetu model services only when needed; login does not generate, saving does not approve and spending follows the agreed scope.

Hetu can provide optional text and image model capabilities to an external Agent. The Agent prepares a fixed plan. You review its material, purpose and budget in the conversation, authorize the scope, and receive results in your work for review.

Local reading, editing and saving do not require a model connection. Keep the external Agent client's account and fees separate from those of a generation service.

## Choose how to use text and image services

| Option | Where to configure it | Account and costs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hetu's optional model service | Connect under Settings → Model service | Uses your Lime Cloud account and allowance |
| Your own compatible provider | Local model settings for endpoint, key and model | Uses that provider's account and billing |
| Other Agent tools | The selected Agent and actual tool configuration | Follows the tool's capabilities and fees |

Check text and image models separately. A text and image catalog does not imply video support. Your Agent uses available external tools for video, sound and editing; see [From preview to finished video](video.md).

## Verify the connection request

Begin the connection in Hetu's model-service settings. Before confirming on the website, make sure its verification code matches the client's, then return to Hetu.

Choose default text and image models separately. The website's account and costs entry opens the corresponding account destination. Check the actual product, amount and payment method before paying or changing a subscription.

Returning from that page does not establish payment success. Refresh the account status and continue based on the actual balance or allowance. The Agent does not make the payment for you.

## Review the fixed plan before execution

Ask the Agent to explain the task in ordinary language:

> Use chapter one and the confirmed character profile to prepare two dialogue variants. Tell me which model will receive what material and the cost ceiling. Do not send the whole book or generate images.

Check inputs, model, purpose, candidate count and budget. For images, also review reference roles and aspect ratio. Confirm permission to send external source material to the chosen service.

If you only want planning, say “prepare the plan without paid generation.” A prepared plan and an executed model call are different results.

## Continue within the approved scope

After you explicitly approve scope and cost, the Agent records authorization through the tools and continues within it. You do not have to return to the client to approve each image again.

More candidates, another model, new external material or reaching a spending limit require another decision. Installing a Skill or connecting an account does not authorize unlimited calls.

You still decide whether to adopt and finalize returned candidates. A successful call is not creative approval.

## Insufficient balance and unknown outcomes

For insufficient balance, check the conversation's allowance or top-up information and verify the account and amount. After payment, inspect the original plan rather than creating the same task again.

After a timeout, query the original plan and provider job. It may still be processing, and another job can incur another charge. Canceling local tracking does not prove external cancellation or a refund.

## Keys for a custom provider

Keys belong in local private storage, not manuscripts, scripts, asset notes or Agent readbacks. Do not put them in prompts, screenshots or support reports.

Custom providers run through the local Core and follow their own accounts and terms without consuming Lime Cloud allowance. See [Privacy and permissions](privacy-and-permissions.md) and [Troubleshooting and recovery](troubleshooting.md).
