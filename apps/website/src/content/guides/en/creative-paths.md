# Choose a project starting point

## Start with what you need

Begin with a fragment even without a finished bible, outline or title. Keep ordinary file work in its original tool; import selectively when you want to develop it. Scripts and videos need no placeholder upstream work.

An independent author, a screenwriter and a small production team may arrive with very different material. Begin with what you have. Your taste and creative judgment stay at the center; structure helps you manage the work.

## A new manuscript

Tell your external Agent the idea. Let it discuss directions, organize details and save a proposal. Review its scope and proposed folder, then return to Hetu to read the result. Manual creation and editing remain available.

Try a small premise: “A lighthouse keeper hears her missing brother’s voice on a radio that has not worked in years.” Decide which emotional question interests you before asking for alternatives. Follow the [Quick start](getting-started.md) for the actual controls.

## An original screenplay

An original screenplay can begin with a scene and a character goal. You do not need to invent a source novel or extract plot evidence from a manuscript that does not exist.

Scripts are independent works with their own episodes and revisions. Tell your Agent the characters, conflict and what you want to read first. Let it return a complete script, then read the action and dialogue and give feedback on a specific passage. No placeholder novel or shared IP is required. See [Develop and review a screenplay](screenplays.md).

For an existing Fountain or DOCX screenplay, inspect the available import path and supported formats in [Formats and delivery](formats-and-delivery.md).

## Your own book, adapted for screen

Select one source revision and try one scene. Give the character an immediate goal and an obstacle, then develop visible action or audible information around the original intention.

Keep your adaptation separate from the manuscript. A scene can compress time or add dialogue, but those choices should be inspectable. The [adaptation guide](adaptation.md) covers the current source-based route.

## A licensed property

Permission to adapt is not necessarily permission to upload the full manuscript to a third-party service or redistribute it in a handoff. Record the edition, intended use and relevant rights information before sharing source material.

For a small studio working with a rights holder, clarify what a screenwriter, an external Agent and a production partner may each receive. Hetu records declarations; it does not certify ownership or provide legal clearance.

## A public-domain story

An older original work may be public domain while a modern translation, annotated edition or set of illustrations remains protected. Identify the actual edition you use.

For example, a reinterpretation of an Austen story can start from an identified public-domain text while keeping a studio’s new scene choices and visual references separate. Rights may vary by jurisdiction, so check the material and intended distribution.

## An existing draft from another tool

Opening a Hetu workspace continues that workspace. Importing a manuscript or screenplay brings external material into a project. Those are different actions.

Inspect structure, order and conversion losses before confirming an import. A file extension alone does not establish its format. See [Formats and delivery](formats-and-delivery.md) for the current scope.

## Existing boards or footage

Give your Agent the material and goal: “Use these three clips to explore a confrontation. Do not generate new picture yet.” Video has its own place in the creative library. Connect a fixed script or source when useful without creating empty novels or scripts.

Return to watch an animatic, a simple 3D preview when needed, or an actual edit. Point to pacing, picture, sound, subtitles or continuity. See [From preview to finished video](video.md).

## What you can test here

The homepage shows original creative examples, revision comparisons and current Electron screenshots. It creates no local project and does not adopt or finalize work. Use the desktop client for your own novel, script or video.

Your Agent handles your own novels, scripts and videos within the scope you authorize; return to review the same results. Keep the sample demo separate from your local work and actual video. The starting material and handoff requirements differ across these routes.
